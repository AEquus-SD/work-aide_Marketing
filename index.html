Email Marketing

3.0 MAINTENANCE, REVIEW, AND TAKEDOWN
3.01 Listing Review & Refresh **Frequency: Every 48 hours.** User (You)
3.02 Actions During Review Check all platforms for accuracy (especially Craigslist). Refresh or re-post Craigslist ads as needed to maintain visibility. Respond to any direct inquiries. User (You)
3.03 "Off-Market" Notification Immediate email/verbal notice from **Broker/Listing Agent**. Broker/Agent
3.04 Immediate Takedown **Within 1 hour** of notification (unless instructed otherwise for marketing purposes). User (You) / Jon Agsalud
3.05 Final Takedown Log into **Buildium** and **SDMLS** and officially change the status to "Leased" or "Off-Market." User (You) / Jon Agsalud
3.06 Remove from all Sites Delete the posting on every single platform: Facebook, Instagram, Craigslist, Rental.com, Apartments.com. User (You)
